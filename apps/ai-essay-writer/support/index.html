Essay result looks wrong?

Try a more specific topic and review your selected length, academic level, tone and style. AI output varies and should be checked before use.

AI Chat response is incomplete?

Add context, ask a narrower question or rephrase the prompt. AI systems may occasionally return incomplete or incorrect responses.

Essay History missing?

Check whether app storage was cleared, the App was reinstalled, or Android backup/device migration changed local application data.

Ads or privacy choices?

Advertising and consent behavior can depend on region, network status, Google Play services, device privacy settings and the current AdMob/UMP configuration.

Need to clear local writing?

Use the App’s available Settings/history clearing controls. You can also clear the App’s storage through Android settings if appropriate.

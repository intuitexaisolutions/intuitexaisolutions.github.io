Use AI responsibly

A starting point, not an authority.

AI-generated content can contain inaccurate, incomplete or unsuitable information. Review the output, verify important facts and follow applicable academic or workplace rules before submitting or publishing generated material.

Academic useFollow your school, university or institution’s rules for AI assistance and original work.
Professional useVerify facts, tone and context before relying on generated material.
Sensitive informationAvoid entering confidential, financial, medical or highly sensitive personal data into AI prompts.
AI limitation

Generated essays and chat responses may be incorrect or incomplete. The app is a writing assistant, not a substitute for professional, academic or factual verification.

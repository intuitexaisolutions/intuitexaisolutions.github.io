This policy is written specifically for the Android iLauncher product and should be kept synchronized with the shipping release and Google Play Data Safety form.

01

Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to iLauncher: iOS 27 Launcher, an Android launcher published by Intuitex AI Solutions / INTUITEX AI SOLUTIONS (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED.

It describes information used for launcher functionality, installed-app organization, personalization, optional widgets, privacy tools, advertising, Premium and support.

02

Default launcher and installed applications

When you choose iLauncher as your default Home app, it needs access to information about applications installed on your device so it can display and launch them. This may include app names, package identifiers, icons and related launcher metadata.

This information is used for Home screen rendering, App Library categorization, Smart Search, icon/title customization, Hide Apps and related launcher features. The product is designed to use this information primarily on-device rather than as a remote inventory of your installed apps.

03

Launcher preferences and local storage

Home screen layouts, folders, dock choices, selected themes, icon size, renamed labels, hidden-app choices, App Lock settings, wallpaper choices and similar preferences may be stored locally on your device so the launcher can remember your configuration.

Clearing application data or uninstalling the App can remove local settings, subject to Android backup and restore behavior.

04

Wallpapers and selected images

If you choose a personal image as a wallpaper, Android may provide access to the media item you select through a system picker or supported storage API. iLauncher uses that selected content to apply or preview the requested wallpaper.

The App does not need unrestricted access to all personal files merely to use a user-selected wallpaper where modern Android picker APIs are available.

05

Widgets and device information

Widgets may display information such as time, date, calendar-style date presentation, battery status and storage capacity. Battery or storage widgets may read device status values needed to display that information; this does not mean the App reads the contents of your personal files.

06

Optional weather location

If you use a location-based Weather widget and grant location permission, iLauncher may access approximate or precise device location as needed to request weather relevant to your area. You can deny or revoke location permission in Android settings.

Core launcher functionality does not require location permission.

07

Hide Apps and App Lock

Hide Apps and App Lock configuration is intended to be stored locally. If biometric authentication is offered, Android’s biometric framework performs the biometric match. iLauncher does not receive or store your fingerprint, face template or other biometric template.

App Lock is a convenience/privacy feature and should not be treated as a high-security vault or substitute for Android’s device lock.

08

Google AdMob and consent

The free experience may display advertising using Google AdMob. Depending on region, device settings, consent choices and Google’s SDK configuration, advertising services may process advertising identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, device information, ad interactions, consent signals and diagnostics.

Where required, a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent flow or another compliant privacy interface may be used to manage advertising choices.

09

Firebase Remote Config

iLauncher may use Firebase Remote Config to control eligible app configuration such as feature availability or whether a Premium entry point is shown. Remote configuration is not intended to upload your Home screen layout, hidden-app list or personal wallpaper to the publisher.

10

Premium and Google Play billing

Optional Premium subscriptions are purchased through Google Play. Google handles payment credentials, billing, renewal and account-level subscription management. The App receives purchase/entitlement information needed to determine whether Premium features should be available.

11

Support communications

If you contact support, we receive information you voluntarily provide, such as your email address, message, screenshots and device/app details included to diagnose an issue.

Support: intuitexaisolutions@gmail.com.

12

Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the App, such as Google Play, AdMob, Google consent services, Firebase Remote Config, weather providers where used, and infrastructure required by enabled features. Providers process information under their own terms and the publisher’s technical configuration.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention or protection of legal rights.

13

Retention and deletion

Local launcher preferences remain until you change them, clear app data or uninstall the App, subject to Android backup behavior. Third-party providers may retain information according to their own documented retention policies.

For privacy questions about information controlled by the publisher, contact intuitexaisolutions@gmail.com.

14

Children's privacy

iLauncher is a general device-personalization utility and is not designed to knowingly solicit personal information from children in violation of applicable law. A parent or guardian may contact us if they believe a child has provided information inappropriately.

15

Security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information involved, but no application, device or network service can guarantee absolute security.

16

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when permissions, widgets, advertising SDKs, Premium functionality, privacy controls or other material product behavior changes. The effective date shown on this page will be updated when the policy is revised.

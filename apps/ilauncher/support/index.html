How do I set iLauncher as default?

Open iLauncher and follow the Home-app prompt, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Default apps → Home app and select iLauncher. Menu names vary by manufacturer.

How do I switch back?

Open Android default-app settings and choose your previous launcher or another installed Home app.

An app is missing from App Library/Search

Refresh/restart the launcher and confirm the app is actually installed and enabled. Android visibility rules, work profiles and manufacturer restrictions can affect what a launcher can display.

Widget is not updating

Check battery optimization, network access for weather, and any permission required by the specific widget. Some manufacturers restrict background updates aggressively.

Wallpaper will not apply

Try selecting the image again through the system picker and confirm Android allows iLauncher to set wallpapers. Some devices provide separate Home and Lock Screen wallpaper controls.

App Lock / biometric not working

Confirm your device has a supported screen lock and biometric method enrolled. Biometric matching is handled by Android system authentication.

Ads or privacy choices

Advertising can depend on region, consent, network conditions and Google services. Where available, use the privacy-options entry in the App to review consent choices.

Premium not recognized

Confirm you are signed into the same Google account used for the purchase, then use the restore/recheck purchase option where available. Google Play controls billing and renewals.

Privacy & permissions

Launcher access should be understandable.

A launcher needs access to information that ordinary apps may not use, such as installed-app metadata. iLauncher’s privacy page explains what is needed and why.

Installed-app informationUsed to render the Home screen, App Library, search, labels, icons and app-management features.
Wallpaper / selected mediaSelected images can be used for wallpaper customization through Android-supported pickers and APIs.
Biometric authenticationHandled by Android system APIs; the app does not receive your fingerprint or face template.
Weather locationIf you enable location-based weather and grant permission, location may be used for the weather widget.
AdvertisingThe free experience may use Google AdMob and regional consent controls.
PremiumGoogle Play handles subscription purchases and account billing.
